# Explorer Man — learnings (living playbook)

Updated every run from vidIQ (YouTube) and Metricool (TikTok) data. Judge only videos 3+ days old.

## Current playbook (as of 2026-11-21)

1. **The Shorts feed is everything.** 86–90% of views come from the Shorts feed (17,418 of 20,295, Oct 24–Nov 20); search 5.7% (1,154, still growing), related videos 3%, subscribers 2%. The feed's first test decides reach.
2. **Frame 1 decides the video.** Engaged-view rate (engagedViews ÷ views) is 44–59% on every Short that reached 1,180–1,600 views vs 17–23% on the flops (Ocean Depth 77/443, Water Ball 84/369), which stalled near 400. Frame 1 = the most shocking true image, with the key number or claim already big on screen, mid-action. No fades, black frames, title cards.
3. **Plateau: nothing has passed ~1,650 views.** All 11 best Shorts sit at 1,220–1,650 (Nov 19 pull, Oct 22–Nov 18: Smallest Thing 1,645, small earth BIG SUN 1,608, 7 Planets 1,522, SS United States 1,495, Starship 1,457). Breaking it is the goal of every test. Search keeps growing (1,038 views, ~5.5% of traffic).
3b. **Engaged-view rate is rising but views are not (confirmed Nov 19):** the newest Shorts have the channel's best openings (Sugar cube 69%, Mars–Jupiter 67%, Leonids 63% engaged) yet stall lower (1,066 / 945 / 1,307 views). Their average % viewed is also lower (Sugar 59%, Mars–Jupiter 52% vs 72–85% on the 55–60 s Shorts): the 61–75 s length loses people mid-video, so keep a new visual every 2–3 s and a mid-way tease (tentative). A good first second is necessary but no longer sufficient: the feed's next test is probably completion/rewatch and shares, so push loops, payoffs and share triggers (tentative).
4. **Space and "you vs. huge/tiny" scale win; lean ~2 of 3 Shorts that way.** Top 8 = 6 space/scale + Lightning + SS United States. Ocean/water/blue-whale/health Shorts did worst (45–443 views).
5. **Timely news + a famous reference can win in any field** (tentative, 1 video): SS United States reef ("longer than the Titanic", news that week) got 1,225 views and the best engaged-view rate (59%), despite being an ocean topic.
6. **Bold counter-intuitive claims break out in the niche** (tentative, outside data): "You Never Actually Touch Anything" (SaturnNova) hit 1.67 M views in 2 days (vidIQ trending, Nov 16). Myth-busting or "impossible-sounding but true" statements in frame 1 and the share line.
7. **Cliffhanger questions with an extreme number can win** (tentative, revised Nov 17): Leonids ("What Happens When Sand Hits Earth at 44 Miles PER SECOND?") has the channel's best engaged-view rate (63%, 714/1,133) and most shares (5) after 2 days; older cliffhangers without a concrete number (Lightning 48%, Voyager 47%) were average. Statement hooks still strong (Betelgeuse 58%, SS United States 59%). Alternate; when using a question, put an extreme number or concrete stake in it.
8. **Viewers who stay, watch** (67–115% average viewed on the top 8), so the middle and ending work; fix the opening, not the middle.
9. **Shares and comments are near zero** (10 shares and 9 comments total across all videos; likes 1.5–2.9%; Starship's on-screen question got the most comments, 3). Every video: one honest share trigger ("Send this to someone who…"), one one-word comment question on screen + in the description, and a pinned-comment question.
10. TikTok per-post metrics from Metricool return empty (again Nov 21) and Metricool's YouTube per-video metrics are empty too; vidIQ is the only per-video signal, and its credits ran out Nov 21 (refill Dec 15). Until then judge by Metricool daily channel views only.
10b. **Possible slump after Nov 15 (tentative, Metricool daily views):** 1,650–2,580 views/day Nov 12–15 vs 200–570/day Nov 16–18 (Nov 19–20 not reported yet). Could be reporting lag or the feed testing newer Shorts less; re-check when vidIQ refills. Keep leaning on the proven formula (space + extreme-number question in frame 1) until per-video data returns.
11. **Long-form: only space-scale ladders get reach** (Nov 18): Powers of Ten 710 views / 87% engaged (mostly suggested traffic) vs Deck of Cards 23 and Journey to Earth's Center 18. Make Wednesday long-forms space/scale "ladders" (rung by rung, each to scale) with a timely hook, key claim on screen from frame 1 (no fade-in), and a chapter every 20–35 s. Niche outliers agree ("A Tour of the ENTIRE Universe" 45×, "How Many Earths Fit Inside Jupiter?" 20×). (tentative, 3 videos)
12. **Everyday-object scale models are the niche's breakout format right now** (tentative, outside data, Nov 19): vidIQ Shorts outliers this month: "Sun vs All Planets: Which Is Heavier?" 566K (41×), "The Sun is just a grapefruit?" 516K (19×), "If Earth Were a Grain of Sand, How Big Is the Universe?" 434K (13×). Tested Nov 19.
