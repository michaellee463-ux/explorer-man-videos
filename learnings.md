# Explorer Man — learnings (living playbook)

Updated every run from vidIQ (YouTube) and Metricool (TikTok) data. Judge only videos 3+ days old.

## Current playbook (as of 2026-11-17)

1. **The Shorts feed is everything.** ~90% of views come from the Shorts feed (11,340 of ~12,600 views, Oct 18–Nov 15); search ~3%, subscribers ~2%. The feed's first test decides reach.
2. **Frame 1 decides the video.** Engaged-view rate (engagedViews ÷ views) is 44–59% on every Short that reached 1,180–1,600 views vs 17–23% on the flops (Ocean Depth 77/443, Water Ball 84/369), which stalled near 400. Frame 1 = the most shocking true image, with the key number or claim already big on screen, mid-action. No fades, black frames, title cards.
3. **Plateau: nothing has passed ~1,650 views.** All 11 best Shorts sit at 1,130–1,625 (Nov 17 pull, Oct 20–Nov 16: Smallest Thing 1,624, small earth BIG SUN 1,605, Starship 1,375 / 54% engaged, Doomsday 1,320 / 51%). Breaking it is the goal of every test. Search is growing (896 views, ~5% of traffic, up from ~3%).
4. **Space and "you vs. huge/tiny" scale win; lean ~2 of 3 Shorts that way.** Top 8 = 6 space/scale + Lightning + SS United States. Ocean/water/blue-whale/health Shorts did worst (45–443 views).
5. **Timely news + a famous reference can win in any field** (tentative, 1 video): SS United States reef ("longer than the Titanic", news that week) got 1,225 views and the best engaged-view rate (59%), despite being an ocean topic.
6. **Bold counter-intuitive claims break out in the niche** (tentative, outside data): "You Never Actually Touch Anything" (SaturnNova) hit 1.67 M views in 2 days (vidIQ trending, Nov 16). Myth-busting or "impossible-sounding but true" statements in frame 1 and the share line.
7. **Cliffhanger questions with an extreme number can win** (tentative, revised Nov 17): Leonids ("What Happens When Sand Hits Earth at 44 Miles PER SECOND?") has the channel's best engaged-view rate (63%, 714/1,133) and most shares (5) after 2 days; older cliffhangers without a concrete number (Lightning 48%, Voyager 47%) were average. Statement hooks still strong (Betelgeuse 58%, SS United States 59%). Alternate; when using a question, put an extreme number or concrete stake in it.
8. **Viewers who stay, watch** (67–115% average viewed on the top 8), so the middle and ending work; fix the opening, not the middle.
9. **Shares and comments are near zero** (10 shares and 9 comments total across all videos; likes 1.5–2.9%; Starship's on-screen question got the most comments, 3). Every video: one honest share trigger ("Send this to someone who…"), one one-word comment question on screen + in the description, and a pinned-comment question.
10. TikTok per-post metrics from Metricool return empty so far; YouTube (vidIQ) is the only reliable signal.
