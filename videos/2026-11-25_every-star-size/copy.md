How Big Is the Biggest Star? ⭐ Every Star Size Compared

# YouTube description
Our Sun is about 109 Earths wide… and next to the biggest stars we know, it shrinks to a dot. We climb the ladder one star at a time, every rung drawn to scale: Sirius, the brightest star in the night sky (about 1.7× wider than the Sun), the giant Arcturus (about 25×), Aldebaran, the eye of Taurus (about 44×), the blue supergiant Rigel (about 75×), Antares, the heart of the Scorpion (about 700×), Betelgeuse (about 750×, with gas thousands of times thinner than air on average) and VY Canis Majoris (about 1,420×: put it where the Sun is and it would reach past Jupiter's orbit; a jet would need nearly 800 years to fly around it). Then: why nobody knows for sure which star is the biggest, and why the heaviest star known, R136a1, is smaller than Aldebaran.
