If the whole history of the universe were squeezed into one year... what time would we show up?
Midnight, January first. The Big Bang.
Every day on this calendar is about thirty-eight million years. Every second, over four hundred years.
By January eighth, galaxies already exist. The James Webb telescope has seen one.
Then February. March. All through summer... still no Sun.
Our Sun and Earth only form in early September.
Life? Late September. Just tiny microbes.
But the craziest part... is December.
Animals don't explode across the oceans until December seventeenth.
Dinosaurs show up on Christmas Day.
And the asteroid wipes them out on the morning of December thirtieth.
New Year's Eve, eleven P M. Still nobody like us.
Our species finally shows up... at about eleven forty-eight P M.
The pyramids? About ten seconds before midnight.
And if you live to eighty, your whole life fits in less than a fifth of a second. About one blink.
Send this to someone who thinks dinosaurs lived ages ago. On this calendar, it was last week.
Which shocked you more? Dinosaurs on Christmas, or us at eleven forty-eight?
