# Copy — one-off: Olympus Mons, the volcano you might not notice

## YouTube title
This Volcano Is 2.5× Taller Than Everest… But You Might Not Even Notice It 🌋🤯

## YouTube description
Olympus Mons on Mars is the biggest volcano in the solar system. Its peak is about 22 km (72,000 ft) high, about 2.5 times the height of Mount Everest and about twice as high as most passenger jets fly. But it's even more shocking sideways: it's about 600 km (370 miles) across, roughly the size of Arizona, and over 20 times wider than it is tall. A wall of cliffs kilometers high wraps around its edge, and its summit crater is about 80 km wide and 3 km deep. Why so huge? On Earth, the ground drifts over hot spots, which is how Hawaii got a whole chain of volcanoes. Mars's crust stays put, so lava kept piling up in one place, and weaker gravity let it climb higher. Its slopes are so gradual that near the top it just looks like a gentle slope that runs past your horizon, only about 3 km away. You could stand on the biggest volcano in the solar system and not even know it.

Sources: NASA / JPL, ESA Mars Express, Britannica, Space.com, Astronomy.com.
