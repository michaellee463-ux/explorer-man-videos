You vs. Space Without a Suit 🧑‍🚀 You'd Have Under 15 Seconds

## YouTube description
Thrown into space without a spacesuit, you'd black out in under 15 seconds. But you would NOT explode — your skin is stretchy and strong enough to hold you together. Above about 19 km (twice as high as Everest), the air is so thin that water boils at body temperature, and the space station flies about 20 times higher. The saliva on your tongue would boil, and holding your breath could rip your lungs. You wouldn't freeze instantly either: deep space is about −270 °C, but with no air, heat leaves your body slowly. In 1966 a NASA suit tester's air hose came off inside a vacuum chamber. His last memory was the saliva on his tongue bubbling. It took 87 seconds to get the air back, and he recovered with no lasting harm. Get back to air within about 90 seconds and you could survive.

Sources: Scientific American, Smithsonian Air and Space Museum, ESA (via Space.com), NASA astronaut Michael Barratt (Engines of Our Ingenuity), Guinness World Records, NASA (ISS altitude), Britannica.
