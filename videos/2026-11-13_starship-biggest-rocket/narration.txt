This is the tallest, most powerful rocket ever built. Starship stands one hundred twenty-four meters tall. That's taller than the Statue of Liberty, torch and all... by more than thirty meters. It's even taller than the Saturn Five, the rocket that took astronauts to the Moon. At liftoff, thirty-three engines fire together, with more than eighteen million pounds of thrust. That's more than twice the thrust of the Saturn Five. But here's the crazy part. When the booster comes back to Earth, it doesn't land. The launch tower catches it... out of the air, with giant mechanical arms. And on flight fifteen, SpaceX plans to catch both stages of the rocket, for the very first time. Follow for more.
