**Title:** How Big Is Starship, REALLY? 🚀

**YouTube description:**
SpaceX's Starship is the tallest, most powerful rocket ever built. It stands 124 meters (408 feet) tall, which is more than 30 meters taller than the Statue of Liberty and taller than the Saturn V Moon rocket. At liftoff, 33 engines produce more than 18 million pounds of thrust, more than twice the Saturn V. And the booster doesn't land: the launch tower catches it out of the air. On Flight 15, targeted for November 13, 2026, SpaceX plans to catch both stages for the first time.

Sources: Scientific American, Astronomy Magazine, NASA, National Park Service, Space.com.
