# YouTube title
How Deep Have We REALLY Dug? Journey to the Center of the Earth

# YouTube description
The center of the Earth is about 6,370 km below your feet. The deepest hole humans have ever drilled, the Kola Superdeep Borehole, took 19 years to reach 12,262 meters: only about 0.19% of the way there. In this video we travel the rest of the way down, layer by layer: the thin crust, the mantle, the liquid outer core that creates Earth's magnetic field, and a solid inner core about as hot as the surface of the Sun. Then: how long would it take to fall straight through the planet (about 38 minutes), and how long would it take to walk to the center (about 53 days)?
