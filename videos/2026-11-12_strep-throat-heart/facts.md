# Strep throat Short: fact sheet

| Claim in video | Verified as | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| Strep throat is caused by group A strep (*Streptococcus pyogenes*), a bacterium, not a virus | Correct | [CDC clinical guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/group-a-strep/hcp/clinical-guidance/strep-throat.html); [StatPearls: Streptococcal Pharyngitis](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK525997/) |
| Spreads through close contact | Correct ("close contact with an infected person is the most common risk factor") | [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/group-a-strep/hcp/clinical-guidance/strep-throat.html); [AAFP 2024](https://www.aafp.org/afp/2024/0400/streptococcal-pharyngitis) |
| Latches onto cells lining the throat and tonsils | Correct (M protein, pili, capsule mediate adhesion to pharyngeal epithelium) | [Proc. Japan Acad. B review, 2015](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/pjab/91/10/91_PJA9110B-02/_html/-char/en); [StatPearls](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK525997/) |
| Releases toxins that punch holes in cells (e.g. streptolysin O) | Correct: SLO "forms large pores in host cell membranes" of epithelial cells | [Proc. Japan Acad. B review](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/pjab/91/10/91_PJA9110B-02/_html/-char/en) |
| Immune response brings swelling, fever, pain | Correct (inflammatory response; exam shows tonsillar swelling, fever, painful swallowing) | [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/group-a-strep/hcp/clinical-guidance/strep-throat.html); [Proc. Japan Acad. B](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/pjab/91/10/91_PJA9110B-02/_html/-char/en) |
| In some people, untreated strep → antibodies attack the heart (rheumatic fever) | Correct: molecular mimicry between M protein and cardiac myosin; autoimmune reaction to GAS throat infection | [WHO RHD fact sheet](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/rheumatic-heart-disease); [AMBOSS: Acute rheumatic fever](https://www.amboss.com/us/knowledge/acute-rheumatic-fever) |
| Rheumatic fever can scar heart valves for life | Correct: "inflammation and scarring of heart valves", permanent | [WHO](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/rheumatic-heart-disease); [AMBOSS](https://www.amboss.com/us/knowledge/acute-rheumatic-fever) |
| Rheumatic heart disease kills hundreds of thousands each year | Correct: WHO ≈360,000/yr; GBD 2015 ≈319,400 | [WHO](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/rheumatic-heart-disease); [Watkins et al., NEJM 2017 (GBD)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28834488/) |
| Diagnosed with a throat swab; treated with antibiotics | Correct: rapid antigen test or culture; penicillin/amoxicillin; treatment prevents rheumatic fever | [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/group-a-strep/hcp/clinical-guidance/strep-throat.html); [WHO](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/rheumatic-heart-disease) |
