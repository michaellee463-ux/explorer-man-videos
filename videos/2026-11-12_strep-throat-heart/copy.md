**Title:** How a Sore Throat Can Damage Your HEART ❤️🦠

**Description:**
Strep throat isn't just a bad sore throat. Group A strep (Streptococcus pyogenes) is a bacterium that latches onto your throat and tonsils and releases toxins that punch holes in your cells. Your immune system fights back, and that battle causes the swelling, fever and pain.

In some people, untreated strep triggers rheumatic fever: the immune response meant for the bacteria attacks the heart instead, and can scar heart valves for life. Rheumatic heart disease still kills hundreds of thousands of people every year, mostly in low- and middle-income countries (WHO).

Most sore throats are caused by viruses. A quick throat swab tells you if it's strep, and antibiotics treat it and prevent rheumatic fever.
