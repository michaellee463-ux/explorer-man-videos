**Title:** You vs. a Blue Whale's Heart 🐋❤️

**YouTube description:**
The blue whale is the largest animal known to have ever lived: up to about 33 meters long and up to 150 tonnes. Its heart weighs about 180 kg, more than 400 times heavier than yours. And on a deep dive, that heart can slow to just 2 beats a minute. In the time it beats once, your resting heart (60–100 beats a minute) beats 30 to 50 times.

Sources: NOAA Fisheries, Royal Ontario Museum, Goldbogen et al. 2019 (PNAS), American Heart Association.
