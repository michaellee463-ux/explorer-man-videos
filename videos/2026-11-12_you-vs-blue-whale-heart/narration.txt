You, versus the biggest animal known to have ever lived. A blue whale can grow up to about thirty-three meters long, and weigh up to a hundred and fifty tonnes. Your heart is about the size of your fist. Under half a kilo. A blue whale's heart? About a hundred and eighty kilograms. More than four hundred times heavier than yours. But the wildest part is how slowly it beats. On a deep dive, it can slow to just two beats a minute. In the time it beats once, your resting heart beats thirty to fifty times. Follow for more.
