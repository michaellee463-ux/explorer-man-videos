**Title:** You vs. a Sugar Cube: All 8 Billion of Us Fit Inside 🤯

**YouTube description:**
Every human on Earth could fit inside a single sugar cube. Here's how: you're made of atoms, and almost all of an atom is empty space. If an atom were the size of a stadium, its nucleus would be about a pea in the middle, yet that pea holds more than 99.9% of the atom's mass. Squeeze out the space and pack your nuclei together, and you'd shrink to a speck thinner than a human hair. Do it to all 8 billion people and we'd fit in a cube just over a centimeter wide: about a sugar cube.

But it would still weigh as much as all of us: about 400 million tonnes, more than a thousand Empire State Buildings. Nature really does this: a neutron star crushes a Sun's worth of matter into a ball about 20 km across, and one sugar cube of it would weigh about a billion tonnes.

Which blows your mind more: the size or the weight? 👇

Sources: Astronomy magazine, NASA, Space.com, Britannica, Walpole et al. 2012 (BMC Public Health), Bar-On et al. 2018 (PNAS), UN World Population Prospects. The pea is magnified for illustration.
