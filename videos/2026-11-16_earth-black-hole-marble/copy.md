**Title:** What If Earth Became a Black Hole? 🌍🕳️

**YouTube description:**
Crush the whole Earth down to about the size of a marble (less than 2 cm wide) and it becomes a black hole. Not even light, at 300,000 km per second, could escape from its edge. Today a rocket needs about 11 km/s to leave Earth.

The weird part: the Moon wouldn't notice. Same mass, same pull, so it keeps orbiting every 27 days. Black holes don't "suck everything in": from far away they pull like anything else with the same mass.

Crush the Sun and it would be about 6 km wide. (Neither the real Earth nor the Sun is heavy enough to collapse like this.) The black hole at the center of our galaxy weighs about 4 million Suns, and its event horizon would fit inside Mercury's orbit. M87*, the first black hole ever photographed, weighs about 6.5 billion Suns: put it where the Sun is and its event horizon reaches about 4 times Neptune's distance. Light would need about a day and a half to cross it.

Which would you rather fall into: the marble or the monster? 👇

Sources: NASA Chandra / STScI, Event Horizon Telescope (via Physics World, Science News), Max Planck Institute GRAVITY (2023), Sky at Night, NASA planetary fact sheets. Event horizons and orbits in the final scenes are drawn to scale.
