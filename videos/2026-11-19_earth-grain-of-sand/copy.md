**Title:** If Earth Were a Grain of Sand, You'd Be the Size of an ATOM 🤯

**YouTube description:**
Shrink the whole Earth down to a single 1 mm grain of sand and keep everything else to the same scale. You'd be about the size of an atom. The Space Station would orbit about 0.03 mm up, less than the width of a typical human hair. The Moon becomes a speck of dust about 3 cm away, and every human who has ever flown in space, including the record-breaking Artemis II crew in April 2026, has stayed within about 3 cm of the grain.

The Sun becomes a grapefruit about 12 meters away. Jupiter is barely a centimeter wide, 61 m out. Neptune is about 350 m away. Voyager 1, our farthest spacecraft, after 49 years: about 2 km. The nearest star, Proxima Centauri, would be about 3,150 km away, roughly New York to Salt Lake City, and at Voyager's speed the trip would take about 75,000 years. And the Milky Way (about 100,000 light-years across) would stretch about 74 million km: halfway from the real Earth to the real Sun.

Which one broke your brain: the atom or the galaxy? 👇
