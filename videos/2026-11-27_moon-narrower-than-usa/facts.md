## Claims (each with ≥2 independent sources)
1. **Moon diameter about 3,475 km (2,159 mi).** NASA (3,474.8 km mean); Space.com ("2,159.2 miles (3,475 km)"); Futurism; WorldAtlas ("2,159 miles (3,475 km)"); Snopes, citing NASA (3,476 km equatorial).
2. **The contiguous USA is over 4,500 km coast to coast.** Wikipedia "Contiguous United States": greatest distance "2,802 miles (4,509 km), coast-to-coast between Florida and Washington state". compute.py measures 4,648 km between Point Reyes, CA and eastern Maine on the Natural Earth outline. WorldAtlas gives 2,897 mi (4,662 km). On screen: "USA 4,500+ KM". The line drawn on the map is the 4,648 km Point Reyes–Maine great circle.
3. **The Moon is narrower than the USA and doesn't reach coast to coast.** 3,475 ÷ 4,509–4,648 = 75–77% (compute.py). Futurism's page is titled "The Moon Compared to the United States"; WorldAtlas says the US is "almost the same width as the moon" (2,897 mi vs 2,159 mi). The map is an azimuthal-equidistant projection centred on the US, with the Moon drawn at 3,474.8 km × the same px/km.
4. **Even Australia is wider than the Moon.** Snopes: "Australia's width from east to west is almost 4,000 km"; "Australia is indeed wider than the moon, but not by much." compute.py measures 4,045 km from westernmost Western Australia (113.8° E) to Cape Byron (153.6° E) on the Natural Earth outline, vs the Moon's 3,475 km. The map is drawn at the same scale.
5. **Ceres is the biggest object in the asteroid belt, about the size of Texas (same area).** Britannica: Ceres is "equivalent in volume to a sphere with a diameter of 940 km", and it is the largest asteroid-belt body. NASA JPL "A Texas-sized space rock": "The Texas-sized asteroid Ceres". compute.py: the disk of a 939.4 km sphere covers 693,092 km² vs Texas's 695,662 km² (100%). Texas is 773 mi (1,244 km) wide (WorldAtlas, A-Z Animals). Ceres does NOT fit inside Texas's outline, which is why the narration says "covers about the same area".
6. **Pluto is only about half as wide as the US.** NASA Pluto facts: "Pluto is only about half the width of the United States"; diameter 2,377 km (1,477 mi). Space.com gives 2,370 km. compute.py: 51–53% of 4,509–4,648 km.
7. **Mercury, a whole planet, is just a little wider than America.** NASA planetary fact sheet: Mercury's diameter is 4,879 km; Britannica gives 4,879 km. compute.py: 1.05–1.08× the coast-to-coast width.
8. **Ganymede, a moon of Jupiter, is bigger than Mercury.** NASA Ganymede facts: "about 3,270 miles (5,260 kilometers)… It's bigger than Mercury and Pluto." Britannica: "diameter of about 5,270 km… larger than the planet Mercury." The on-screen 5,268 km is NASA's 2,634.1 km radius × 2.
9. **Mars is about one and a half Americas wide.** NASA Mars fact sheet: mean diameter 6,779 km (Britannica gives 6,779 km). compute.py: 1.46–1.50×. The on-screen silhouettes use the projected US outline width of 4,604 km.
10. **Earth is less than three United States across.** NASA Earth fact sheet: mean diameter 12,742 km. compute.py: 2.74–2.83×.
11. **The Moon's surface is about 37.9 million km², almost four times the whole United States and more than all of Africa.** Universe Today: "37.9 million square kilometers". Snopes: "37.94 million square kilometers". Space.com: 38 million km². WorldAtlas: "approximately four times as much surface area" as the US. US total area is 3,796,742 sq mi = 9.83 M km² (US Census). compute.py: 3.86×. Africa is 30.37 M km² (GeographyWorlds), so compute.py gives 1.25×. Space.com and Universe Today: the Moon is less than Asia (0.85×). I don't claim more than Asia.
12. **Flattened into one circle, the Moon's surface would be twice as wide as the Moon.** This is geometry: the area 4πr² equals π(2r)². compute.py gives a 6,950 km disk. The on-screen "peeled" disk is a real Lambert azimuthal equal-area map of the whole sphere, whose outline is exactly a circle of radius 2r.
13. **Pluto's entire surface is about the size of Russia.** Using NASA's diameter, compute.py gives 4π(1,188.3)² = 17.74 M km² vs Russia's 17.1 M km², so Pluto is about 4% larger. GeographyWorlds says "Pluto is only about 3% larger than Russia" (17.65 vs 17.1). CommonplaceFacts: 17,646,012 km² vs 17,075,200 km², "slightly beats out Russia". FunFactz flags "Pluto is smaller than Russia" as a debunked myth. We say "about the size of", never "smaller". The on-screen Russia silhouette is an equal-area (Lambert) projection at the same km scale as Pluto's flattened skin.
