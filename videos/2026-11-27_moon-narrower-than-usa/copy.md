The Moon Is Narrower Than the USA 🌕🤯 (Drawn to Scale)

## YouTube description
Drop the Moon on a map of the United States, to scale, and it doesn't even reach coast to coast: about 3,475 km wide vs over 4,500 km for the lower 48. Even Australia (about 4,000 km) is wider than the Moon. Ceres, the biggest object in the asteroid belt, covers about the same area as Texas. NASA says Pluto is only about half as wide as the US. Mercury, a whole planet, is just a little wider than America, and Ganymede, a moon of Jupiter, is even bigger than Mercury. Mars is about one and a half Americas wide, and Earth is less than three United States across.

But here's the twist: the Moon is narrower, not smaller. Peel its surface and lay it flat, and it would cover almost four United States (about 37.9 million km²). That's more than all of Africa, and as one flat circle it would be twice as wide as the Moon itself. And Pluto's entire surface? About the size of Russia.
