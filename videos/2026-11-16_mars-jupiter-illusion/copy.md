**Title:** Mars and Jupiter Look Like They're Touching… They're 368 Million Miles Apart 🤯

**YouTube description:**
This week, an hour before sunrise, Mars and Jupiter sit barely more than a pinky's width apart, high in the southern sky (closest Nov 15–16, 2026). But it's an illusion. Mars is about 121 million miles (195 million km) away, and Jupiter is four times farther, about 489 million miles (787 million km). The gap between them is about 368 million miles, nearly four times the Earth–Sun distance.

The light you see from Mars left it about 11 minutes ago. Jupiter's light left about 44 minutes ago. So you're seeing two different moments in time, side by side. Jupiter is about 21 times wider than Mars (almost 9,000 Mars would fit inside it by volume), and even from four times farther away it shines about 12 times brighter.
